Track Clear Test

Builds every track exactly as the game does, checks that no building stands within 5 m of the barriers, that every visible barrier is solid (no barrier inside the drivable area), and fires downward rays across the full road width every few metres, starting 500 m up. Anything that is not part of the track itself (buildings, trees, grandstands, crowds, rocks...) sitting on or over the road fails the test. Bridges and tunnels over the road are allowed.
